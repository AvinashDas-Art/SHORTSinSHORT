## Business description

SHORTSinSHORT is a curated discovery and editorial platform for independent, regional and global short cinema. It helps viewers discover publicly available short films through language, genre, mood, duration, festival and thematic curation.

The platform does not download, re-upload, host or sell third-party video files. Public films are shown through official embed players, including YouTube. The original creators, producers and lawful rights holders retain the underlying copyright, channel ownership and monetisation rights.

All publicly embedded films remain free for every visitor. Payment is not charged for playing, unlocking or accessing any YouTube video.

## Why the payment gateway is required

Razorpay is required for an optional viewer membership called **SHORTSinSHORT Cinema Club**, introduced at **₹5 per week**.

The membership pays only for SHORTSinSHORT's own editorial and community services:

1. A weekly **Curator's Five** programme with an original curator note and suggested viewing order.
2. A monthly online **Cinema Room** discussion.
3. **Member Jury** participation in choosing future themes and spotlights.
4. **Cinema Notebook** essays, interviews and filmmaking resources owned by or expressly licensed to SHORTSinSHORT / Equal Tales Entertainment Pvt Ltd.

Cinema Club membership does not unlock third-party films. The public catalogue and every embedded film remain accessible without payment.

SHORTSinSHORT currently does not charge filmmakers a film-submission or listing fee.
